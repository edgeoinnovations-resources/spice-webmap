# Spice Changed the World: a detective's atlas

**Live map:** https://edgeoinnovations-resources.github.io/spice-webmap/

A companion webmap for *Spice*, a book in progress by Marc Aronson and Marina Budhos,
made for grade 9 students at the American School of Dubai. It follows the authors as they
look for traces of a trade that is hard to see today, and treats their travel photographs
as evidence.

- **Story:** an opening page, then seven sections (Opening, Cases 1–5, Epilogue) with a paced, scroll-driven map
- **Explore:** 23 map layers and a monsoon month dial
- **Evidence:** 21 photographs with Then / Now / Gone notes, OPTIC and SCRAP questions, certainty labels and photo metadata

This is a draft built from the authors' 2026 book proposal. Text will change as the book is written.
